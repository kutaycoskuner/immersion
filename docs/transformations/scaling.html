Step 4: self experimentation and comprehension

Change sxs_xsx​, sys_ysy​, szs_zsz​ on the diagonal and drag the bar.

a factor of 111 changes nothing; between 000 and 111 the cube shrinks, above 111 it grows

a factor of 000 flattens the cube onto a plane; a negative factor mirrors it

the scaling is about the origin: the origin stays, and a point twice as far from it moves twice as much

unlike a translation it also changes directions (w=0w = 0w=0): the diagonal multiplies them as well
