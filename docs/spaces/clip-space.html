II. description · camera → clip

Camera space to clip space

The step after world to camera: the input is camera space -- the eye at the origin, looking down −z-z−z, as world to camera left it. The point ppp is typed in camera space here (in front of the eye: z<0z < 0z<0).

The camera sees only a part of it, its view volume: between a near and a far plane, and only so far to the sides. fov, near and far are settings of the camera itself; they make the see-through pyramid in the viewport, from the eye (its tip) through near (the screen) to far.

The projection matrix PPP takes every point into clip space: four numbers (x,y,z,w)(x, y, z, w)(x,y,z,w). Clip space is 4D, it has no shape to draw -- so the viewport stays in camera space and shows what clip space is for: the clip test. What the camera can see is bright, what it cannot is grey, like the two boxes beyond far and behind the eye.

Step 1: the frustum

A perspective camera sees a frustum: a pyramid with its tip at the eye, cut off at near and far. Its opening is the field of view (fov); the aspect (width / height) is 111 for now.

Step 4: the clip test

Inside the view volume means every coordinate lies between −w-w−w and www -- one test against the point's own www, no divide needed. Clipping uses it to throw away or cut what the camera cannot see:

Behind the eye www is negative: such points fail before anything is divided -- that is why the test comes before the divide.

No animation here

Clip space is 4D and cannot be drawn, so there is nothing to animate: the viewport stays in camera space and the panel shows only P pP\,pPp. The squeeze into a cube is the next step, the divide by www (clip to NDC), which has the bar.

Not yet 2D

Depth goes along in every step so far: PPP has an inverse, and the divide keeps zzz. The picture becomes 2D only when the screen reads xxx and yyy as the place in the picture; zzz goes to the depth test (what is in front).

put ppp beyond far: its zzz grows past www
