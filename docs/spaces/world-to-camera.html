II. description · world → camera

Camera space

The camera is one more object in the world (the white frame in the viewport), placed like the boxes, with its own axes: xxx to its right, yyy up, zzz backwards. It looks down its own −z-z−z.

Camera space (view space) is the camera's local space: every point measured from the eye, along the camera's own axes. A point straight ahead has x=y=0x = y = 0x=y=0 and a negative zzz.

World to camera is again 3D to 3D: nothing is flattened, depth is kept, and the way back exists. It is the step before the projection, which then only has to look along −z-z−z from the origin.

Right-handed (OpenGL, three.js), the camera looks down −z-z−z; left-handed (DirectX) it looks down +z+z+z -- the R / L switch of the hand in the gizmo.

Self experimentation and comprehension

only move the eye and target by the same offset: RRR stays, only VVV's last column changes

put the target straight in front: eye (0,0,5)(0, 0, 5)(0,0,5), target (0,0,0)(0, 0, 0)(0,0,0) -- VVV is just a move by −5-5−5 along zzz

a point behind the camera gets a positive zzz: the projection will have to throw it away

put the eye right above the target: the up is replaced, the picture turns
